Put your favorite photos on your wrist. WristFrame shows a photo full screen on your watch, and nothing else.

- Holds up to 4 photos. Press Up and Down to switch between them.
- Choose photos from your phone in the app's settings, including iPhone HEIF photos.
- Each photo is cropped to fill the screen and dithered to the watch's 64 colors.
- Photos are stored on the watch, so they stay available when your phone is not connected.
- Private: photos are converted on your phone and sent straight to the watch. Nothing is uploaded.

For the Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.

Open source: https://github.com/lanrat/WristFrame
